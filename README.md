## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🧠 CRM System for a Real Client (Custom Solution)
This is a fully customized CRM system built for a real-world client operating in a distributor-centric B2B environment. I led the development end-to-end, from requirement gathering to deployment, designing a scalable architecture tailored to complex client workflows.
#### 💼 What It Does

This system goes far beyond traditional CRM functionality, acting as an **operational backbone** for managing distributors, contracts, and client communications in a regulated business environment.

#### 🔑 Core Features
- **Client Management for Distributors:** Add, manage, and track clients with detailed relationship data.
- **RTP Management:** Generate and manage Receive Transmission Protocol (RTP) documents, with differentiated flows for Admins and Distributors.
- **Contract & Document Management:** Upload, view, and digitally sign contracts, instruction manuals, and maintenance reports.
- **Trial Setup & Tracking:** Initiate business trials, assign related companies, and monitor trial statuses.
- **Maintenance Reporting:** Generate maintenance reports and sign them digitally for compliance and tracking.
- **User & Role Management:** Administer employees, assign roles, and control access with **role-based permissions** for Admins, Accountants, Distributors, and Supplier Staff.
- **Company & Business Management:** Add and manage businesses, link them to companies, and store key contact data.
- **Internationalization:** Multi-language support with a UI that adapts to a global user base.
- **Real-Time Notifications:** Toast messages for user feedback and action confirmation.
- **Authentication & Security:** JWT-based login with custom error pages (403 Unauthorized & 404 Not Found).
- **PDF Generation & Signing:** Create downloadable PDFs of contracts and reports, and enable digital signing for legal workflows.

#### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Backend:** Java, Spring Boot, JPA, JWT, Flyway, MySQL, JWT Authentication
- **Frontend:** React, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Other:** Cloudinary (media management), Docker, AWS EC2 (deployment)
